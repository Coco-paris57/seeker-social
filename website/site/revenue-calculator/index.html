About the estimate

Read this before you trust any number.

How accurate is the estimate?

It's a range built from typical London rents and nightly rates, adjusted for your area and size. Finish, floor, views, outdoor space and the exact street all move the number, which is why we follow up with a free appraisal using real comparables.

Why not 365 nights of short stays?

Because in Greater London that isn't allowed without planning permission. Short stays are limited to 90 nights per calendar year. The estimate assumes short stays up to that limit and furnished mid-term stays for the rest of the year.

Does the estimate include tax?

No. Figures are before income tax. Since April 2025 furnished holiday lettings no longer have a special tax regime, so speak to your accountant about your own position.

What about guaranteed rent?

Guaranteed rent is a fixed figure we agree per property. It's usually lower than the managed estimate, because we take on the risk of empty nights and the running costs. Ask for an offer and we'll show you both.
