Questions

Mid-term stays, explained.

How long can I stay?

From one month to around six months. Tell us your dates and we'll confirm availability and a monthly price.

What's included in the monthly price?

The furnished home, utilities, Wi-Fi and council tax. Mid-stay cleaning and linen changes can be added.

Can my company pay and get an invoice?

Yes. We invoice companies, relocation agents and insurers directly.

Is this a tenancy?

No. Mid-term stays are furnished accommodation for people living temporarily in London, for work, relocation, study or while their own home is unavailable. We'll confirm the right agreement for your situation.

Tell us where and when. We'll reply with options and a monthly price within one working day.
