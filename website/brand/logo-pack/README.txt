SEEKER ESTATES — LOGO PACK (October 2026)

WHICH FILE TO USE
  svg/     Vector files. Use for print, signage, designers, Canva/Adobe. Scale to any size.
  png/     Transparent images for documents, emails, websites, presentations.
           -green  = for light backgrounds   |   -ivory = for dark/green backgrounds
           logo-on-ivory / logo-on-green = ready-made with background (2400 px wide)
           mark = the keyhole arch on its own   |   app-icon = rounded square icon
  social/  Square profile pictures (1080 px) for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, WhatsApp,
           Google Business Profile. Safe for circle crops.
           -with-name = keyhole arch with "Seeker Estates" underneath (recommended)
  stacked  svg/seeker-estates-stacked-*.svg and png/seeker-estates-stacked-*-2048px.png:
           the arch above the name, for square spaces (posts, signage, stickers).

COLOURS
  Mayfair Green  #0F3D2E   RGB 15 61 46     (main colour)
  Ivory          #F6F1E7   RGB 246 241 231  (backgrounds)
  Clay           #C8582F   RGB 200 88 47    (accent; use #A9441F for small text)
  Sand           #EADFCB   RGB 234 223 203
  Ink            #13221C   RGB 19 34 28     (text)

FONTS (free, Google Fonts)
  Headlines: Fraunces (semi-bold, italic for accent words)
  Text & buttons: Plus Jakarta Sans

DO
  Keep clear space around the logo of at least the height of the keyhole arch.
  Use the green logo on light backgrounds and the ivory logo on green or photos.
DON'T
  Stretch, rotate, recolour (outside the colours above) or add shadows/outlines.
  Place the green logo on dark or busy photos.
