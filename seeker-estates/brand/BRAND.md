# Seeker Estates — social brand system (v1, 10 Sep 2026)

**Positioning:** Exceptional stays. Professionally managed. — mid-term (91+ nights) first, short-let second.
**Voice:** Professional. Premium. Reliable. Never cheap, never desperate, never argumentative, never misrepresent.
**Faceless brand:** no founder or staff on camera. On-screen text, voiceover (optional), real ambient sound.

## Colour
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Navy | #0B1F35 | Grounds, text blocks, title cards (from the logo) |
| Ink | #011729 | Text on light |
| Mist | #DCE8F2 | Light ground, carousel pages (from the logo's background) |
| Brass | #C9A24D | One accent only: location tags, rules, labels. Never large areas |
| White | #FFFFFF | Text on navy, logo tile |

## Type (Google Fonts, free)
- **Lora 700** — hooks, headlines, the title card. Matches the serif wordmark in the logo.
- **Poppins 500/400** — labels, dates, captions on screen, location tags.
- No third face. No script fonts. No all-caps body text.

## Logo
- `logo-transparent.png` — navy mark on transparent, use on light grounds.
- `logo-tile-white.png` — mark inside a white rounded tile, use on navy/dark/video.
- Never stretch, recolour or put the mark over busy footage without the tile.

## Video (Reels / TikTok / FB Reels)
- 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264, ≤ 60 s. Hook text on screen by 0.5 s.
- Safe zone: no text in the top 15% or bottom 25%.
- Text block: Lora 60 white on a navy bar, max 880 px wide, centred at ~40% height.
- Location tag: Poppins 30 ink on brass, ~68% height: `E14 8NT · CANARY WHARF · JUBILEE / ELIZABETH`.
- Timed-walk format: black chip top-right, "WALK TIME" + mm:ss, one continuous take sped 4×.
- Monday anchor opens with `title-sting` (2.5 s), then the week's 3–5 items, each with its tube stop tag.
- Audio: real ambient sound or a cleared library track. Never trending audio (business accounts can't).

## Carousels (IG / FB, TikTok photo mode)
- 1080×1350 (4:5). Mist ground, navy 4 px border inset 60 px, logo top-left, label top-right in brass.
- Cover: Lora 96 headline, 5–7 words per line, ink navy for the first two lines then slate.
- Inside pages: same frame, Lora 64 headline + Poppins 34 body, max 45 words per page.
- Last page: the CTA and the caveat line (landlord posts: "General information, not legal advice").

## Captions
- First line = the hook. Search words written plainly ("where to stay near ExCeL London").
- 3–5 hashtags. No links in the caption. CTA line last.
