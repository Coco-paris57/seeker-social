The Bayeux Tapestry is in London for the first time in 950 years.

It opened at the British Museum yesterday and runs until 11 July 2027 — the biggest cultural loan the UK has received in a generation. Nearly seventy metres of embroidered linen telling the story of 1066.

Ticketed and timed, so book ahead. Tottenham Court Road, Holborn and Russell Square are all under eight minutes on foot.
