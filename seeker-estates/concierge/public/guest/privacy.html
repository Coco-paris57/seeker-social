Privacy notice for the guest chat

This page explains, in plain English, what happens to the information you share in this chat during your stay.

Who is responsible

Seeker Estates LTD runs this chat and is the data controller for it under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

What we collect

  • Your messages and the replies you receive, with the time each was sent.
  • Your booking surname and arrival date, when you verify your booking. This is optional from an apartment QR code, and required if you open the chat from a general link. We use them to find your booking. Once it is found, the chat is linked to it, so the team can see your name, the apartment and your stay dates alongside the chat.
  • Which apartment's QR code you scanned, so answers are about the right apartment.

There is no account and no password. Please do not send card details or other sensitive information you do not need to share.

Why we use it

To answer questions about your stay and to fix problems you report. Our lawful bases are performing the booking contract with you and our legitimate interests in looking after guests and the apartments.

The AI assistant and the Seeker team

Answers are drafted by an AI assistant. It runs on Claude, a service provided by Anthropic, which processes your messages on our behalf as a processor. The Seeker team can read every chat, can review answers before they are sent, and replies in person when needed. Messages written by a person are labelled "Seeker team".

The assistant does not make decisions about your booking. Anything that needs a decision is passed to the team.

Where booking details come from

Your booking details come from our booking system, Smoobu, which holds the reservations made through the site or channel you booked with.

How long we keep it

Your chat is erased a set number of days after the last message. You can erase it immediately at any time: open the menu in the chat and choose "Delete my chat". "Leave chat on this device" only removes the chat from your device; the chat itself is still erased on the same schedule.

Cookies

The chat uses one strictly necessary cookie. It keeps you in your chat on this device, and it is removed when you delete or leave the chat. There are no tracking or analytics cookies and no third-party scripts.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your chat (access), ask us to delete it (erasure), or object to how we use it. To do so, contact Seeker Estates through the channel you booked with, for example your Airbnb or Booking.com messages.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk.
