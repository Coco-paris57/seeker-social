## Findings
1. **The decline started in May, not on 10 Oct.** Booking.com went from 13–23 bookings a month in March–April to 0–3 a month from June. It is a 5-month slide, so look for something that changed around May (ranking, review score, promotions, content, a price or availability setting), not a recent outage.
2. **It is not price.** What Booking.com guests paid fell over the same period (£195 in May bookings → £118–131 in Sep–Oct), so the property did not get more expensive for guests while volume fell.
3. **Airbnb only partly filled the gap.** Airbnb bookings on the flat (both listings) were 1–4 a month in June–October. October stays: Airbnb 22 nights, Booking.com 4. November has 11 booked nights (all Airbnb), December 5.
4. **No future Booking.com stays at all.** Booking.com guests here book late (median 7.5 days before arrival), so this matters most for the next 2 weeks: 38 of the next 60 nights are open (22 Oct – 20 Dec), at a PriceLabs average of £223, which Booking.com shows as about £268 before promotions.
5. **Early bookers cancel.** 16 of 34 bookings made more than 30 days ahead were cancelled (47%), against 1 of 24 made within a week (4%). Overall 21 of 67 (31%). The non-refundable rate exists (−10%), so guests are choosing the flexible rate.
6. **Min stay is not the blocker.** Open nights carry a 2-night minimum (22 nights), 3 nights (14) or 1 night (2).

### What the extranet adds
1. **Since August the flat is available, cheaper than peers and well reviewed, but not booked.** Availability for search is now above the peer group (32–34% vs 24–29%), ADR is ~30% below peers (£120–132 vs £170–186), the review score is 8.4 (above peers) and the page score 92–97%. Search views recovered to ~60% of peers (22–24k a month). Yet Booking.com bookings were 0, 2 and 1 in Aug, Sep and Oct.
2. **So the break is after the search result**: either guests don't click (search card: main photo, name, the total shown in search), or they open the page and don't book (cleaning fee and total at checkout, policies, house rules, room/bed setup). Price per night and reviews are not the explanation.

4. **The review score is not the problem**: 8.4 with 12 of 15 reviews at 7+, flat since August.

### Conclusion
Every visibility and discount lever is already on, the page and reviews are good, and the flat is available more than its peers, yet Booking.com produced 0–2 bookings a month since August. More discounts are not the answer:
1. **Guests now pay about half the PriceLabs price on Booking.com.** Booking.com guests here book late (median 7.5 days), so a typical guest gets Last Minute 15% + Genius 10–20% + Mobile 10% + Domestic 10%. Paid ADR fell to ~£120 (Oct) against a PriceLabs price around £223 for open nights and peers at ~£186. Even after the +20% markup, Booking.com is now the cheapest channel for this flat (Airbnb paid ~£160).
2. **The new deals added since August (Last Minute 26 Aug, Early Booker 26 Sep, Domestic and EEA 2 Oct) produced 2 bookings between them.** Bookings did not respond to deeper discounts, so they only cut the rate on bookings that would have come anyway.
3. **Black Friday 30% does not stack** with Genius or any other promotion; Booking.com shows the largest single discount (Corentin, 21 Oct, from the extranet note). It is bookable only 19 Nov–2 Dec. At 1.2 × PriceLabs × 0.70 it nets about 0.84 × the PriceLabs price, which is more than guests pay today under the stacked deals (about 0.54 ×).
4. What remains unexplained is the drop between search and booking. That needs the monthly page-visit and bookings trend and the 30-day funnel.

### Recommended changes (Booking.com extranet, this property)
- Switch off Domestic and EEA country rates and Early Booker. Keep the Black Friday Deal: it does not stack, it runs for two booking weeks only, and it gives a deal badge.
- Keep Mobile 10% (it produced 42 bookings) and Last Minute 15% only if Genius is cut back to Level 1 (10%): untick Genius Level 2 & 3 and Level 3.
- Keep the Visibility Booster (it costs nothing unless a booking comes).
- Check the cleaning fee and total shown at checkout for a 3-night stay.
