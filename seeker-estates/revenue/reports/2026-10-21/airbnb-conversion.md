## What it means (units we keep)

1. **St John's Wood: guests see it but don't click.** It is on page 1 more than any other unit (69%), but only 7.3% of searchers click through, against 42% for the portfolio. Those who open the page book at 6.2%, the best rate of all. The problem is the search card: cover photo, title and the total price shown in search (a 4-bed at £500+/night shows a large total). Fix the first photo and title first; test the search price (for example, the weekly/monthly discount display or a lower cleaning fee) before touching the nightly rate.
2. **Shepherd's Bush 2 (3090701): guests click but don't book.** 61% click through but only 0.79% book, against 4.09% on Shepherd's Bush 1 for the same flat. The twin lists about 8.7% higher (pricelabs.md, comp-west.md) and its amenity record is different. Align its price and content with listing 1, or compare the two pages photo by photo.
3. **Waterford Court: the Airbnb page converts well** (4.40% listing → booking, +1.56 pt vs similar listings) since it started getting bookings in late September, then dropped in the latest week. Its low occupancy comes from too few channels (Airbnb only) and too few reviews, not from a weak listing.
4. **Airbnb conversion is above similar listings on every unit.** Together with the demand review (Booking.com: 0 bookings since 10 Oct), this points to Booking.com as the main leak, not Airbnb listing quality. The extra Airbnb discount (~23%, channel.md) is probably part of why Airbnb converts so well: on the strongest converters (Shepherd's Bush 1, Marylebone, Waterford) it could be trimmed first.
5. **Mile End** is weak at both steps (31.8% click, 1.2% book) but is handed back after 13 Dec.
